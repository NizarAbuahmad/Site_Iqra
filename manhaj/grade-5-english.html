الفصل الأول

مُرَحَّبًا بِعَوْدَتِكُمْ

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Present simple with before/after/when — e.g. When Jameela gets home, she plays with her cat.
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative.
    • Identifying people in their immediate surroundings or in pictures from a short, simple description of their physical appearance and clothes.
    • Describing someone's physical appearance in a basic way.

عَرْضُ الْمَواهِبِ

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Comparative and superlative adjectives — e.g. My mum is shorter than me. She's the shortest person in our family!
    • be good at — e.g. I'm good at baking a cake.
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative.
    • Recognising familiar key words and phrases in short, basic descriptions.
    • Describing people — e.g. She's very friendly.

بِالْأَمْسِ وَالْيَوْمَ

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Past simple — e.g. Did you have a phone ten years ago? That computer was really big!
    • could/couldn't — e.g. When I was one, I could walk but I couldn't talk.
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative.
    • Extracting factual information from short, simple dialogues or stories about past events.
    • Identifying the context of short, simple dialogues related to familiar everyday situations.
    • Talking about past events or experiences, using simple language.
    • Expressing ability or lack of ability in relation to basic everyday actions.
    • Function: Asking for information — e.g. I'd like some information about the Science Museum, please.

هَيَّا نَسْتَكْشِفُ!

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • will/won't — e.g. I'll get him a present. I won't get him a book.
    • How...? — e.g. How deep is the Grand Canyon?
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative.
    • Identifying specific information in short, simple dialogues in which speakers make arrangements to do something.
    • Identifying key information about future plans in short, simple dialogues.
    • Talking about plans for the near future in a simple way; giving an opinion in a structured discussion; telling a simple story.

إِلَى السُّوقِ

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Relative clauses — e.g. It's the man who we saw earlier. We're in the shop where my brother works.
    • have to/don't have to — e.g. I have to save some money.
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative.
    • Identifying key information such as prices, times and dates in a short description.
    • Describing basic differences between two pictures.

حَيَواناتٌ مُهَدَّدَةٌ بِالْخَطَرِ

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Past continuous — e.g. The shark was looking for small sea animals.
    • Past continuous vs past simple — e.g. I was watching the koalas when the lion escaped.
    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative, if told slowly and clearly.
    • Identifying basic factual information in short, simple dialogues or stories on familiar everyday topics.
    • Identifying specific information in short, simple dialogues, if there is some repetition and rephrasing.
    • Understanding some details in longer dialogues on familiar everyday topics, if guided by questions or prompts.

  1. المفردات والقواعد
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • should/shouldn't — e.g. You should go to the doctor's! You shouldn't go to school.

    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Following the sequence of events in a simple story or narrative, if told slowly and clearly.
    • Identifying basic factual information in short, simple dialogues or stories on familiar everyday topics.
    • Identifying specific information in short, simple dialogues, if there is some repetition and rephrasing.

    نتاجات التعلّم
    • Present perfect — e.g. I've read Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. I haven't seen the film.

  2. الاستماع والتحدث
    نتاجات التعلّم

  1. المفردات والقواعد

    المفاهيم والمصطلحات
  2. الاستماع والتحدث

    • Identifying people in their immediate surroundings or in pictures from a short, simple description of where they are and what they are doing.
